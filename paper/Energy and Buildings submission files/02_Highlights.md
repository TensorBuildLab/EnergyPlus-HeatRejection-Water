# Highlights

- Eight cooling tower evaporation models compared inside EnergyPlus simulations
- Errors of simple models follow the state of the air leaving the tower
- Simple models overpredict in hot, dry air and underpredict in cold air
- Default EnergyPlus method differs from Poppe by up to 38 % for a data center
- Open-source EnergyPlus plugin makes the physics-based models available to all
