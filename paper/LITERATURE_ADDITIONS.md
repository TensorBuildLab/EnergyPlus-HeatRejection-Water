# Literature additions (response to referee: "literature review too thin")

Compiled 2026-10-31. 20 entries were added to `references.bib` under the "Literature-review additions" banner.

How each entry was checked:
- The DOI was resolved through `api.crossref.org/works/<doi>` (and doi.org), and the authors, title, year, journal, volume and pages were matched.
- The abstract or a summary of it was read from one of these sources: Crossref/OpenAlex abstract, publisher page, PubMed, OSTI/ETDEWEB, IIR FRIDOC, a university research portal, or search-indexed ScienceDirect abstract text.

ScienceDirect blocks direct fetching. For several Elsevier papers, the summary below is therefore built from abstract text indexed by search engines or by OSTI, PubMed or university portals, not from the full paper. These are marked †. Check the exact numbers against the PDF before quoting them in the manuscript.

Candidates suggested in the brief:
- **Already cited:** Qureshi & Zubair (both the 2006 and 2007 papers are in the bib).
- **Real but not added:** Hajidavalloo et al. 2010 (doi:10.1016/j.enconman.2010.01.005). Secondary sources disagree on which way its evaporation-loss result goes, and the abstract could not be read.
- **Real but not added:** Picardo & Variyar 2012 (doi:10.1016/j.enconman.2011.12.016). It is about the packed-height calculation, not evaporation.
- **Real but not added:** Lemouari et al. 2009 (doi:10.1016/j.enconman.2009.02.002). It is an experimental study of packing flow regimes, which is only marginally relevant.

All three are verified, so they can be added if wanted.
