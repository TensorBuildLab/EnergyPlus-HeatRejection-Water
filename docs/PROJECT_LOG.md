Chronological record of what was built, decided and found (2026-10-31), so nothing depends on chat history.
Author: Liang Zhang (University of Arizona), with Claude Code.

## 1. Starting point
- `CONCEPT_NOTE.md`: Study 2 of the NSF CAREER proposal. Gaps in EnergyPlus 26.2: evaporation physics, chemistry-based
  blowdown, hybrid/adiabatic dry coolers. Liquid-cooled IT is out of scope (LBNL PR #11667).

## 2. Tower water plugin (accounting mode)
- Design decision: evaluate all evaporation methods on **the same EnergyPlus operating point** each timestep, so differences
  isolate evaporation physics; the plugin does not change the thermal solution.
- Eight methods: LAT, ROT, LF, EPSAT (EnergyPlus replica), MSAT, BRAUN, POPPE (reference), POPPE1 (`docs/METHODS.md` §3).
- Air flow (`METHODS.md` §4): Merkel towers report `Cooling Tower Fan Speed Ratio` (not "Air Flow Rate Ratio"); in free
  convection EnergyPlus reports 0 air flow while rejecting heat, so the sized free-convection flow is used. VariableSpeed
  (YorkCalc/CoolTools) towers report a capacity fraction as air-flow ratio in fan-off/cycling; replaced by a PLR-weighted
  average of min-speed and free-convection air.
- Near-pinch handling: EnergyPlus' Merkel tower (Braun c_s linearisation, lowest fan speed meeting setpoint) puts an oversized
  tower's exit air within <0.5 % of the saturated enthalpy at the hot-water temperature; Poppe (Le_f < 1, evaporated water
  enthalpy) can then be infeasible. Status `ADJUSTED`: re-solve with the smallest air factor (1.01–1.5) that is feasible.
  Mean factor 1.01–1.03 in the 14-climate study; evaporation insensitive (+2 % air → <0.1 %).
- Fog: exit air supersaturated; annual fog hours count liquid > 0.1 g/kg at site pressure (reporting threshold).
- Chemistry: constituent limits, LSI limit (closed form N = 10^((LSI_max − pH + pHs(1))/1.9)), amorphous silica solubility
  (Fournier 1977); modes fixed / constituent / dynamic; offline scenarios in `tools/scenarios.py`.
  **Water sources and treatment limits in `data/water_sources.json` are placeholders.**

## 3. Verification
- 48 unit tests (`tests/`), psychrometrics vs PsychroLib, Poppe vs an independent height-wise BVP (`tools/verify_poppe.py`,
  < 1e-4 %), EPSAT replica vs EnergyPlus (< 0.4 %), rating-mode agreement Merkel/Poppe/e-NTU (< 0.5 K).
- Merkel-number definition: the rearranged Poppe equations integrate on the local water flow; this is 0.5–0.7 % larger than
  K/m_w,in. Documented, not an error.
- References checked against publisher records (`docs/REFERENCES_CHECK.md`); corrections applied; open: source of the
  empirical LSI A–D constants (likely Carrier 1965, not Standard Methods), exact Kloppers & Kröger 2005 claim, ATC-105 node values.

## 4. Models and weather
- Data-center model (`models/DATACENTER_MODEL.md`): 1 MW IT, 2 data halls, CRAH, 3×50 % Carrier 19XR VSD chillers (10 °C CHW),
  3-cell Merkel tower (approach 3.9 K, range 5.6 K at 0.4 % WB), CW reset WB+3.9 K (18–29.4 °C), climate-specific sizing
  (`models/set_design_days.py`). PUE 1.24–1.28. Generated by `models/build_datacenter_idf.py`.
- Hybrid variant (`--heat-rejection hybrid`): 3×50 % FluidCooler:TwoSpeed on one node, setpoint = node DB + 5.6 K (23–40 °C),
  3×50 % Trane CVHE ReformulatedEIR chillers (valid to 41.11 °C leaving condenser), hard-sized air flows (design air rise
  8 K; 10 K made low-speed UA unsizeable at > 600 m elevation), fan 0.02 W/W.
- Weather: 14 TMYx 2011–2025 sites (ASHRAE 0A–6A) + Phoenix TMY3 (`weather/`, from climate.onebuilding.org).

## 5. Results (details in the paper and `results/`)
- Climate map (fixed tower, full load): saturated-exit methods −18 to +11 % vs Poppe (over in hot–dry, under in cold–humid);
  empirical −39 to +117 %; Lewis factor alone −4 to +2 %.
- Data center, 14 climates: EnergyPlus default −3.7 to −9.3 % annual evaporation vs Poppe; LF (as documented) +4 to +64 %;
  evaporation 1.05–1.65 L/kWh_th; WUE 1.66–2.63 L/kWh; chemistry −13 to +22 % on makeup.
- Hybrid, 14 climates × 7 strategies: wet above 28 °C keeps chillers in range (≤5 h/yr outside; dry-only up to 3,285 h);
  14–100 % less water than the tower; always-wet in Riyadh +9 % vs tower; 100–240 L water per kWh electricity saved.

## 6. EnergyPlus issues found (`bugreports/README.md`, reproductions included)
1. Idle towers book evaporation/drift/blowdown (stale air-flow ratio; `report()` runs after `calculateWaterUsage()`).
2. Merkel tower books no evaporation in free convection (air-flow ratio 0 while Q > 0).
3. VariableSpeed tower uses a capacity fraction as air-flow ratio in fan-off/cycling.
4. `LossFactor` applied as a fraction although documented in %/K → 100× evaporation (confirmed by simulation: 20 %/K).
5. Fluid-cooler air flow autosized about three orders of magnitude too large (Q[W]/ΔT×4 as m³/s).
Not yet confirmed on v26.2; not yet filed upstream.
